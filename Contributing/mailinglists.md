## Mailing lists

- **[public-{{ site.cgname_lc }}cg@w3.org](mailto:public-{{ site.cgname_lc }}cg@w3.org):**
    the group's primary mailing list. Group members are automatically signed up. The
    [archives](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-{{ site.cgname_lc }}cg/)
    are publicly visible, and anyone can post a mail.

- **[internal-{{ site.cgname_lc }}cg@w3.org](mailto:internal-{{ site.cgname_lc }}cg@w3.org):**
    the group's member confidential mailing list. Group members are automatically signed up. The
    [archives](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Member/internal-{{ site.cgname_lc }}cg/)
    are visible to W3C members only, and only persons with a W3C user account can post a mail.
